Gallery

One room. Five moods.

Laurel, Golden Hour, Blush, Monochrome, Regal. Same 2,000 square feet — the linens, lighting, centrepieces, and arch all change as one set.

These are our design renderings — the real room is going up now. Follow the build on Instagram.

01The presets

i.

Laurel

Sage and cream linens, eucalyptus laid down the centre of every harvest table, pillar candles grouped in threes. Soft green uplighting carries the palette up the walls.

ii.

Golden Hour

Antique brass candlesticks, amber uplighting, and more candle flame than overhead light. The room holds that 7 PM warmth for the whole evening.

iii.

Blush

Dusty pink linens with cream cloth napkins, rose gold charger plates, and soft florals on every table. Made for bridal showers, engagements, and anything that will be photographed a hundred times.

iv.

Monochrome

Black and white linens, silver accents, clear acrylic centrepieces under clean white light. The sharpest of the five — pick it when you want a milestone birthday to read like a gala.

v.

Regal

Jewel-tone linens, gold charger plates, crystal in the centrepieces, and the arch dressed to match. The full-production option for sangeets, receptions, and hundred-guest birthdays.
