Dossier · The Laurel Room Sheet · The room Photographs · Zero Opening · 2027

One room. Five looks. Zero photos — on purpose.

The room is unbuilt, and we won’t pretend otherwise with renders. Drawings are honest: they show what’s decided, not what’s dressed up. Photos arrive when the paint does.

photos when the paint dries, not before

The floor plan, before the floor exists.

Drawing A-101, pinned here in its laurel state. Fifty feet by forty, twelve harvest tables, the arch inside the top wall, step-free at the door. The five looks below recolour everything soft in this room — the walls don’t move.

Drawing A-101 Plan view Seats 125 Aug 2026

Banquet seats 125 at twelve harvest tables — that’s the layout drawn above. Theatre, cocktail, and floor seating for mehndi nights are layout options too; each gets a confirmed capacity when we open in 2027 (construction estimate), not an invented number now.

The five looks, in swatches instead of renders.

A look commits the uplighting colour, the linens, the centrepieces, and the arch dressing to one palette. Here is each one as the materials we’ll actually buy — not a render of a room that doesn’t exist yet.

i

Laurel

sage and cream, eucalyptus down every table
  • sage linen
  • cream runners
  • eucalyptus
  • pillar candles

The uplighting goes soft green, and the twelve harvest tables get sage linen with cream runners, eucalyptus laid down the middle, pillar candles between the place settings. The arch is dressed in greenery to match. The tables, the layout, and the 20-plus parking spaces stay exactly where the drawing puts them.

ii

Golden Hour

7 PM warmth, held for the whole evening
  • amber uplight
  • antique brass
  • taper candles

The LED uplighting turns amber and the overhead light steps back — antique brass candlesticks and taper candles do most of the work. The centrepieces and the arch dressing warm up to match. Nothing structural moves: the same tables, the same floor plan, the same parking.

iii

Blush

dusty pink on cream, rose-gold at every seat
  • dusty-pink linen
  • cream
  • rose-gold chargers

Dusty-pink linen with cream cloth napkins, a rose-gold charger at each place, and centrepieces and arch florals in the same soft range. The uplighting shifts to a warm pink. Underneath it all, the room is the same drawing: twelve tables, one arch, step-free at the door.

iv

Monochrome

black, white, and silver under clean light
  • black + white linen
  • silver
  • clear acrylic

Linens go black and white, accents go silver, and the centrepieces are clear acrylic under neutral light — no colour wash at all. The arch is stripped back to match. The plan doesn’t change; the same twelve tables just read sharper in this palette.

v

Regal

jewel tones, gold, and crystal — the full production
  • jewel-tone linen
  • gold chargers
  • crystal

Jewel-tone linen, gold chargers, crystal in the centrepieces, and the arch dressed the heaviest of the five looks. The uplighting deepens toward the linen colour. Tables, layout, and parking are untouched — this is dressing, not renovation.
