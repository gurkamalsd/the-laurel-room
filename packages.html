Pricing

Pricing you can read.

Three packages, day rates on the table, 13% HST and nothing hidden after it.

01Time blocks

The day splits in two.

A 5.5-hour block is the unit. Book the morning, the evening, or take the whole day.

  • Block A Daytime — showers, aqiqahs, brunch ceremonies. 10:00 AM–3:30 PM
  • Block B Evening — mehndis, receptions, birthday dinners. 5:30 PM–11:00 PM
  • Full day Morning setup to last song, no turnover in between. 10:00 AM–11:00 PM

On weekends the two blocks book separately — a baby shower at 10 AM, a sangeet at 5:30 PM, same Saturday, same room.
