Welcome

We’re building Brampton a room for the celebrations that never fit.

2,000 square feet for 50 to 125 guests. Bring your own caterer — yes, your aunt’s biryani guy is welcome. Public pricing from $895. The paint isn’t dry because the walls aren’t up: we open in 2027, and founding families book first.
