Your caterer, your playlist, your people.

Most halls hand you a menu and a minimum. We think that’s backwards. Book the room and bring whoever cooks for your family — the caterer your cousin swears by, the aunty who does the sweets, the restaurant that knows exactly how your mother likes the biryani.

Outside catering is the point of the room, not an exception we grant. No in-house menu. No food minimum. No surcharge for serving what you actually eat. If the cooking is happening inside your own family, that works here too.

  • A halal caterer? Bring them
  • Your family’s curry? Serve it
  • Thali for a hundred? We’ll set the table

Two questions, answered properly

Almost everyone arrives with the same two. Whether you can really bring in outside catering, which is the one rule we won’t trade away. And why we’re building for 50 to 125 and stopping there. The rest we write down as we go, in the journal.

Founding families go first.

A short list, free to join, capped at thirty dates. A founding family’s price is locked at the top of the published band for their day — so if costs rise between now and 2027, the number doesn’t. You also get first pick of the calendar, Saturdays included, and an invitation to see the space before anyone’s allowed to call it finished.

If you joined the list when we were advertising 10% off, that still stands — we’re not moving the goalposts on anyone who signed up early.

One email a month at most, and only when there’s something worth showing you.
