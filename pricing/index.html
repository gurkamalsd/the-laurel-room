Ranges you can read without asking — and the reason they’re ranges.

Every band below has two ends, and 13% HST after it — nothing else. If you’re working out what an event space rental costs in Brampton for 50 to 125 guests, this page is our whole answer.

Most 100-guest Saturday evenings here will land between $3,200 and $4,300 all in, HST included.

That is the room, the styling, the staff most people end up adding, and the tax. Your caterer bills you separately, because your caterer is yours.

We haven’t signed a lease yet.

That’s the honest reason these are ranges and not a price list. What a room costs to run depends on the building — the rent, the ceiling height, the parking, how many washrooms it already has. We’d rather show you the band we’re designing to than a number we’d have to quietly walk back.

So every package is written with both ends showing. Where your date sits inside the band comes down to the day you pick and how much of the room you want finished before you arrive.

One more thing about the numbers

These are 2027 dollars, not today’s. We priced for the year you’d actually be standing in the room, not for what a room costs to run this week.

Nothing here is priced by the head.

Three things move the number: the day of the week, how long you need the room, and how finished you want it when you walk in. Nothing else. No seasonal surcharge, no per-head fee, no charge for serving what you actually eat.

Every package on this page allows outside catering. Any caterer, any cuisine, no in-house menu and no food minimum. If the cooking is being done by your family, self catering is fine too — the kitchen arrangement is a requirement we’re taking into the lease, not an upsell.

A $250 hold, fully refundable, until there is a room to stand in.

The whole schedule, printed. Nothing here changes after you sign, and nothing here is negotiated over the phone.

Reservation fee
While the lease is unsigned, $250 holds your date and is fully refundable for any reason. It converts into the retainer only once the lease is signed and you have stood in the room.
Retainer
After that, 25% at signing. We call it a retainer rather than a deposit because it pays for taking your date off the market. Then 50% of the balance at 90 days out, and the final balance at 14 days with your final headcount.
Damage deposit
Refundable. $500 on The Space, The Styled Experience, The Morning Celebration, The Corporate Retreat and The Short Block. $750 on The Full Celebration and The Mehndi Night. Back to you by e-transfer within 7 business days.
Cancellation
Beyond 180 days, you lose the retainer and nothing more. Between 90 and 180 days, 50% of the contract. Inside 90 days, 100%.
Date transfer
One free move, if you ask more than 120 days out, to any date within 12 months, subject to availability.
Tax
13% HST on everything above, and nothing after it. No service charge, no gratuity added at the end, no fee for the caterer you chose.

Founding families lock the top of their band.

Thirty dates, no more. A founding family’s price is locked at the top of the published band for their day — so if costs rise between now and 2027, the number doesn’t. You also get first pick of the calendar, Saturdays included, and an invitation to see the space before anyone is allowed to call it finished.

If you joined the list when we were advertising 10% off, that still stands — we’re not moving the goalposts on anyone who signed up early.
